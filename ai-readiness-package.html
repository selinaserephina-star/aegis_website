What is a KCP manifest?

The missing layer between your organisation and your AI tools.

KCP (Knowledge Context Protocol) is a structured YAML format that describes what your company does, what it builds, who it serves, and how it works — in a form that AI tools can read directly without needing to be told every session.

Drop knowledge.yaml in your repo root and Claude Code, Cursor, and any MCP-compatible agent picks it up automatically. Place llms.txt on your web root and your site becomes AI-discoverable without a crawl.

Most teams write these by hand once, let them rot, and wonder why their agents give generic answers. The AI-Readiness Package generates yours from what actually exists — your codebase, your website, your public information.
