What is Codebase Intelligence?

The analysis you'd get from a senior architect — agent-drafted, human-reviewed, delivered in days.

Most teams know their codebase has issues. They don't know which ones matter, how bad they are, or where to start. Codebase Intelligence answers all three — quantified, prioritized, actionable.

The agent runs the same toolchain we use for enterprise technical due diligence: Synthesis for indexing, ExoCortex for agentic analysis, Claude Opus for synthesis. Then a senior engineer reviews every finding — catches false positives, adds context, tightens the roadmap. You get three reports you can act on, not a raw AI dump.

The output package drops straight into Claude Code, Cursor, or any agent framework via the included KCP manifest — your AI tools immediately understand what the codebase does and what's wrong with it.
