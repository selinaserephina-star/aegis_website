What is Codebase Intelligence?

The analysis you'd get from a senior architect — run by an agent, delivered in an hour.

Most teams know their codebase has issues. They don't know which ones matter, how bad they are, or where to start. Codebase Intelligence answers all three — quantified, prioritized, actionable.

It runs the same toolchain we use for enterprise technical due diligence engagements: Synthesis for knowledge indexing, ExoCortex for agentic analysis, Claude Opus for synthesis. The difference is it's automated and delivered for a fraction of the cost.

The output package drops straight into Claude Code, Cursor, or any agent framework via the included KCP manifest — your AI tools immediately understand what the codebase does and what's wrong with it.
